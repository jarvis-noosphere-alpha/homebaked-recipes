Borscht Recipe From Scratch: Deep Beet Broth

Classic borscht made from scratch with beef, cabbage and potato. Rich beet broth, tender meat and a swirl of sour cream. Ready in 1 hour 20 minutes.

Prep + cook: 1 hr 20 minServes: 6 servingsAbout 310 kcal

Ingredients

  • 800 g beetroot, peeled and grated
  • 500 g beef brisket, in chunks
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 200 g cabbage, shredded
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 3 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Sour cream and fresh dill, to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the beef in 3 litres of water with the bay leaf for 40 minutes. Skim the foam, keep the broth warm.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot and garlic in oil for 6 minutes, add the tomato paste and cook 1 minute more.
  3. Add the grated beetroot and cook 12 minutes — it should soften but not turn to mush.
  4. Add the potatoes and cabbage, pour in the hot broth, season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook gently for 25 minutes. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and rest for 10 minutes off the heat.
  6. Serve in deep bowls with a spoonful of sour cream and plenty of dill.
Arthur's note. Beetroot cooks faster than cabbage, so it goes in first. For a brighter colour, add the beet at the very end and let it sit.
