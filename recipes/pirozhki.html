Pirozhki With Beef and Egg: Oven-Baked Russian Turnovers

Pirozhki with beef and egg filling: flaky shop-bought pastry, a rich meat filling and a golden egg-wash top. Baked in 25 minutes, better than the ones from the shop.

Prep + cook: 50 minServes: 12 turnoversAbout 290 kcal

Ingredients

  • 400 g minced beef
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 2 eggs, hard-boiled
  • 1 egg for the wash
  • 250 g plain flour
  • 150 g cold butter
  • 150 ml sour cream
  • 1 tbsp flour for the filling
  • Salt, black pepper and caraway seeds

Method

  1. Make the dough: rub the cold butter into the flour with a pinch of salt, add the sour cream, knead for 3 minutes and chill for 20 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion in a dry pan until soft, add the mince and cook until it browns and the moisture evaporates.
  3. Add the flour, season generously and stir for 1 minute. Cool the filling completely.
  4. Chop the boiled eggs and mix them into the cooled filling with the caraway seeds.
  5. Roll the dough into thin rounds, cut 12 circles, add a teaspoon of filling and pinch the edges closed.
  6. Brush with beaten egg, cut a small slit on top and bake at 190°C for 22–25 minutes until golden.
Arthur's note. Let the filling cool to room temperature before closing the pastry, or the dough will soften and leak.
