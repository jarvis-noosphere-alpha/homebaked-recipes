Medovik: Russian Honey Layer Cake

Medovik honey layer cake: 8 thin sponge layers, a tangy sour cream frosting and a drizzle of honey. Rest it overnight and it tastes even better.

Prep + cook: 2 hr 0 minServes: 10 servingsAbout 380 kcal

Ingredients

  • 700 g flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 g honey
  • 150 g sugar
  • 200 g butter
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 300 g sour cream
  • 200 g cream cheese
  • 100 g powdered sugar

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking soda and sugar. Add the eggs, softened butter and honey and knead into a firm dough.
  2. Divide the dough into 8 parts and rest each for 15 minutes.
  3. Roll each part into a thin sheet, poke holes with a fork and bake at 180°C for 7–8 minutes until light brown.
  4. Cool the layers completely — warm sponge will tear the frosting.
  5. Whip the sour cream with the cream cheese and powdered sugar until thick. Spread a thin layer over each sheet and stack.
  6. Cover and refrigerate overnight, then cut with a hot knife and drizzle with honey.
Arthur's note. Poking holes is essential, otherwise the layers puff up and the cake falls apart.
