Sirniki: Russian Cottage Cheese Pancakes

Sirniki are thick, tender cottage cheese pancakes served with berry compote and sour cream. Breakfast in 20 minutes with no flour needed.

Prep + cook: 20 minServes: 4 servingsAbout 260 kcal

Ingredients

  • 500 g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Vanilla extract
  • Butter for the pan
  • Berry compote and sour cream, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the cottage cheese, eggs, flour, sugar, salt and a little vanilla into a thick, slightly grainy batter.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan with a little butter over medium heat.
  3. Shape the batter into thick rounds with wet hands and cook for 3–4 minutes until the edges set.
  4. Flip and cook the other side for 2 minutes until golden.
  5. Keep warm in a low oven while cooking the rest of the batch.
  6. Serve in a stack with berry compote, a spoonful of sour cream and a drizzle of honey.
Arthur's note. Full-fat cottage cheese gives the best texture. Drain watery curd cheese first and the pancakes will hold their shape.
