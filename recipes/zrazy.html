Zrazy: Russian Meatballs in Cream and Mushroom Sauce

Tender Russian meatballs simmered in a creamy mushroom sauce with onions and a hint of garlic. Ready in 45 minutes, perfect with mashed potato.

Prep + cook: 45 minServes: 4 servingsAbout 420 kcal

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef and pork
  • 1 onion, finely grated
  • 2 slices of white bread, soaked in milk
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion for the sauce, diced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Mix the minced meat with the grated onion, squeezed bread, egg, salt and pepper. Shape into walnut-sized meatballs.
  2. Fry the meatballs in butter on all sides until golden, about 6 minutes.
  3. Soften the diced onion and mushrooms in the same pan for 8 minutes until the liquid from the mushrooms has cooked away.
  4. Stir in the flour, cook 1 minute, then pour in the cream and add the garlic. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Return the meatballs to the pan, cover and simmer gently for 20 minutes. Serve with mashed potato or rice.
Arthur's note. Squeeze the bread well and the meatballs stay juicy. Fry them in the same pan you will make the sauce in — the browned bits give the sauce its flavour.
