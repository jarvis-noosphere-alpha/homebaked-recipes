Soft Kefir Buns: Easy Russian Yeast Buns

Soft Russian kefir buns made with tangy kefir, sugar and a little butter. Bakery-soft inside, sugar-dusted outside, ready in under an hour.

Prep + cook: 55 minServes: 10 bunsAbout 240 kcal

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 300 ml warm kefir
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 60 g sugar
  • 50 g butter, softened
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp poppy seeds
  • Honey, to serve

Method

  1. Warm the kefir to 37°C. Mix it with the yeast and 10 g of the sugar, leave for 10 minutes until it foams.
  2. Add the flour, remaining sugar, salt, egg and softened butter. Knead for 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 40 minutes until doubled in size.
  4. Shape into 10 buns, arrange on a lined tray, cover and rest for 15 minutes.
  5. Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with poppy seeds and bake at 180°C for 18–20 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve warm with honey and tea.
Arthur's note. If the dough feels sticky, let it rest 10 minutes and knead again — it will come together. Do not add extra flour.
